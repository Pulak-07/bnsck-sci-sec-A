About Us • The Legacy of Section A

United by Kaptai Lake, Bound by Purpose

Nested amidst the scenic hills of Kaptai and the tranquil expanse of Kaptai Lake, Bangladesh Noubahini School and College Kaptai stands as a symbol of naval discipline, intellectual curiosity, and enduring fellowship.

Established within BNS Shaheed Moazzam, our journey began with 56 passionate students united under HSC 2027 Science Section A. As time shaped our paths, 6 classmates transitioned through transfers, leaving 50 students continuing forward together. Yet their contribution remains an inseparable part of our batch chronicle.
