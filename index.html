About Us: Meet Our 50 Stars Open 3D Constellation

The Legacy of Section A

Nested amidst the scenic hills of Kaptai and the tranquil expanse of Kaptai Lake, Bangladesh Noubahini School and College Kaptai stands as a beacon of discipline, intellectual curiosity, and shared fellowship. Established in the historic naval base of BNS Shaheed Moazzam, our cohort brings together 50 dedicated classmates striving toward the HSC 2027 examinations, guided by our respected Principal Sir, Vice Principal Sir, Section A Class Teacher, and our Senior Lecturers.
