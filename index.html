The Legacy of Section A

Nested amidst the scenic hills of Kaptai and the tranquil expanse of Kaptai Lake, Bangladesh Noubahini School and College Kaptai stands as a beacon of discipline, intellectual curiosity, and shared fellowship. Established in the historic naval base of BNS Shaheed Moazzam, our journey began with 56 dedicated classmates united under HSC 2027 Science Section A. As time shaped our paths, 6 of our classmates moved forward through transfers, leaving 50 students continuing the journey together. Yet, their departure remains an inseparable part of our story, memories, and legacy.

Today, our 50 dedicated classmates strive toward the HSC 2027 examinations, guided by our respected Principal Sir, Vice Principal Sir, Section A Class Teacher, and Senior Subject Lecturers. Though our numbers have changed, the spirit of Section A remains unchanged—a bond built on friendship, perseverance, and memories that will endure far beyond our years at Kaptai.
